# The music: So What'cha Want (Beastie Boys, 1992), a first take, the
# vocals left out. Its loops simplified: the main groove a bar long, the
# organ's part a bar or two, the song built of them. tools/mkmusic.py
# makes build/gen/music.inc of it for music.s, and with --wav a WAV to
# listen to.
#
# From the record: 85.4 beats a minute, straight; tuned 40 cents above
# A 440; the main loop on G with F, A, C, D and F-F#-G (a G7sus blues), a
# tuned kick sliding down from about G1 on 1 and on the and of 4, the
# bass coming in on the e of 2, the and of 3, the e of 4; the hi-hats in
# eighths. The second part (bars 43-49 of the record) round A with D, F,
# C and E, the A reached from the G# below - an organist's smear.
#
# tempo   frames to a tick (a sixteenth), as a fraction; two of them for
#         swing: the ticks on the beat, the ticks off it
# tune    cents above A 440
# wave    a name, its kind and what it needs:
#           drawbars 888000000 [perc2|perc3]  a Hammond's nine drawbars,
#                    16' to 1', 0-8; perc: the percussion's 4' or 2 2/3'
#                    sounding at full (a page for the note's attack)
#           harm a1 a2 ... [clip g]  harmonics' strengths, clipped (fuzz)
#           pulse 50, saw, noise
# inst    a name, "drop n" for a pitch that falls by 1/2^n each frame
#         (a kick 3, a boom 7), "vib depth speed" (the note's step swayed
#         by up to depth 256ths, 0.4 % each, speed 256 a turn a frame: 34
#         is 6.6 Hz, a Leslie at full speed), and its pages, one a frame
#         from the note's start: wave.level (level 1-7) or 0, silent; the
#         last one held, or from "loop" on again and again
# pattern a name and its notes; voice: a voice's notes
#
# In a pattern or a voice: a note (C4, F#5), ^ before one: no new attack
# (legato, the organ's smear), - a rest, ~ the note held on, /n the ticks
# of what follows (2 if not said), @name an instrument, *name a pattern,
# "loop" where the voice starts again once it is through, | between bars
# (each checked to be 16 ticks). A pattern's notes go an octave lower for
# an organ with its 16' drawn.
#
# Levels: the three voices add up, and the TED goes no louder than 1 + 7 -
# what goes over is cut off.

tempo 123/14
tune 40

wave organ  drawbars 888000000
wave orgp   drawbars 888000000 perc3
wave fuzz   harm 1 0.6 0.4 0.2 clip 3
wave pulse  pulse 50
wave noise  noise

inst organ  vib 3 34  orgp.3 orgp.3 orgp.3 orgp.3 organ.3 organ.3
                      loop organ.3 organ.3 organ.3 organ.3 organ.2 organ.2 organ.2
inst bass   fuzz.3 fuzz.3 fuzz.3 fuzz.3 fuzz.3 fuzz.3 fuzz.2 fuzz.2 fuzz.2 fuzz.1
inst boom   drop 7 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3
                   pulse.2 pulse.2 pulse.2 pulse.2 pulse.2 pulse.2 pulse.1
inst kick   drop 3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.3 pulse.2 pulse.2 pulse.1 0
inst snare  noise.3 noise.3 noise.3 noise.2 noise.2 noise.2 noise.1 noise.1 0
inst hat    noise.1 noise.1 0

# ---- drums: the groove, a bar; the break's fill ----
pattern groove
  /2 @kick G2 @hat C5 @snare C5 @hat C5 @hat C5 @kick G2 @snare C5 @kick G2 |
pattern fill
  /4 - - /2 @snare C5 /1 C5 C5 /2 C5 C5 |

# ---- bass: the main loop on G (the boom with it), the second part's on D ----
pattern bass
  @boom /4 G1 /1 - @bass F2 F#2 /3 G2 /1 A2 /2 G2 /1 F2 @boom /2 G1 |
pattern bass-b
  @bass /4 D2 /1 - D2 D3 /3 D2 /1 F2 /2 G2 /1 G#2 /2 A2 |

# ---- organ ----
# the intro: G held, the Leslie turning, F and back
pattern swell
  /12 G4 /2 ^F4 ^G4 | /16 ~ |
# over the groove: a G7sus call and its answer
pattern riff
  /3 - /1 F4 /2 ^G4 /2 - /2 C5 /2 ^D5 /4 ~ |
  /2 F5 D5 C5 A4 /6 G4 /2 - |
# the second part: A smeared up to from G#
pattern theme
  /1 G#4 /7 ^A4 /2 G4 F4 /4 D4 |
  /1 G#4 /5 ^A4 /2 C5 /4 ^D5 /2 C5 A4 |
# the break: A held alone
pattern hold
  /1 G#4 /15 ^A4 | /16 ~ |

# the form: intro (2 bars, organ alone) - and from here again and again:
# groove (4), groove with the organ (8), the second part (4), the break
# (2), groove with the organ (8)
voice @organ *swell
      loop /16 - - - -  *riff *riff *riff *riff  *theme *theme  *hold
      *riff *riff *riff *riff
voice /16 - -
      loop *bass *bass *bass *bass  *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass
      *bass-b *bass-b *bass-b *bass-b  /16 - -
      *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass *bass
voice /16 - -
      loop *groove *groove *groove *groove  *groove *groove *groove *groove
      *groove *groove *groove *groove  *groove *groove *groove *groove
      /16 - *fill
      *groove *groove *groove *groove *groove *groove *groove *groove
