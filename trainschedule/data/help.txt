= HOW TO PLAY
:[UP] [DOWN] READ ON  [FIRE] CLOSE

You run the signal box. Trains come in at the edges of the map and run to
a timetable. Set their routes from signal to signal, so that each of them
stops at the right platform, on time.

= ROUTES
Move the frame onto a signal and press fire: the signals a route from it
can go to start blinking. Fire on one of them, and the route is set - it
turns green, and the next train to come up to the signal takes it. Routes
can be set ahead, one after the other.

Fire on the signal of a route to take it back, and choose another or none
- even when a train has taken it, as long as the train has not passed the
signal and can still stop.

A train at a platform turns round: set a route from the signal behind it.

= THE SCORE
10 points for every stop. Fewer for every minute late, for the wrong
platform, for stopping at a red signal and for braking before one.

70 per cent of a map's points earn a star - and a star opens the next map.

= THE BOARD
At the foot of the map a small book: the trains, where each is going next
and whether it is on time. Fire on a train for its timetable.

= JOYSTICK AND KEYS
Fire counts when it is let go. Held, with the stick pushed, it is a
command.
