# The teams' flags for the title, read by tools/mkflags.py: each team's
# name (title.s's, in its order), then its flag as 24 rows of 24
# multicolour pixels, a letter each (a pixel two hires pixels wide, so the
# flag is 48 x 24 on the screen). Drawn after each flag's official
# construction sheet, stripes and crosses to its measures; its emblems
# (stars, suns, arms) pixel by pixel at its size, as far as 2-to-1 pixels go.
#
#   W white   R red     N navy    B blue    S sky     K black
#   Y yellow  O gold    G green   D dark (the title's green)  M maroon
#
# A flag is 6 x 3 cells of 4 x 8 pixels, and a cell shows four colours:
# dark, white, the flag's second shared colour (red, unless another leaves
# fewer pixels wrong) and one of its own. Each flag here keeps to that -
# mkflags.py says which pixels it would have to change if one did not.
# Brazil's and Portugal's greens are the title's dark green for it: green,
# yellow, blue and white, or green, red, yellow, white and blue, would not
# go into their cells otherwise.
